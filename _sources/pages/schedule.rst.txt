**Workshop Format**

This workshop combines plenary talks, technical sessions, and a panel
discussion to provide a comprehensive overview of hybrid quantum, HPC, and
AI methods for scientific applications.

* **Talks** – Experts from industry, academia, and government research
  laboratories present recent advances in quantum algorithms, system
  integration, and software for hybrid quantum-HPC-AI workflows.
* **Panel Discussion** – The National Lab Strategies session includes a
  panel discussion with national laboratory leaders on near-term directions
  for quantum-HPC-AI integration.
