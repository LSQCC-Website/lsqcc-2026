2nd Annual Workshop on Large-Scale Quantum-Classical Computing (LSQCC)
######################################################################

Hybrid Quantum, HPC, and AI Methods for Scientific Applications

Quantum computing is advancing rapidly, and its integration with
high-performance computing (HPC) and artificial intelligence (AI) is
becoming increasingly important for enabling practical hybrid
computational workflows. In a growing number of domains, research is
progressing toward quantum advantage—with quantum computation already
addressing problems that strain exact classical methods and
increasingly competing with state-of-the-art approximate approaches.
These combined methods offer new opportunities for accelerating
progress in scientific areas such as physics, chemistry, materials, and
optimization by leveraging quantum computing together with established
classical and AI-based techniques.

The 2nd Annual Workshop on Large-Scale Quantum-Classical Computing
(LSQCC) brings together recent research on quantum algorithms, system
integration techniques, and emerging hybrid workflows that incorporate
HPC and AI components where beneficial. The workshop highlights
technical challenges, recent developments, and near-term opportunities
for effectively combining quantum computing with complementary
classical and AI-based techniques.

This is the second edition of LSQCC; the first LSQCC was held at SC25.
The 2nd LSQCC will be held as a full-day workshop at SC26 on
**Monday, 16 November 2026, 9:00am–5:30pm CST, Room W179a**.
